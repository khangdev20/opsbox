Restore, safely

Restoring never overwrites live data by default. opsbox restores into a new database or folder next to the original, then you switch over when you are happy.

  1. Pick a backup and choose Restore to a copy
  2. Check the copy in Containers or Files
  3. Swap it in, or delete the copy

Encryption

Backups are encrypted before they leave this server. Keep the recovery key somewhere safe: without it a backup cannot be opened.
